# Roster

* [Fokker Dr.I](../aircraft/fokker-dr1.md) — triplane turn-fighter (the Red Baron).
* [Fokker D.VII](../aircraft/fokker-d7.md) — inline all-rounder, altitude specialist.
* [Albatros D.III](../aircraft/albatros-d3.md) — sesquiplane hard-hitting energy fighter.

# Identity & insignia

- **German:** no roundel. **Iron Cross (Eisernes Kreuz)** — black flared-arm cross on a
  white field — through most of the war; replaced by the straight-armed **Balkenkreuz**
  (thick black cross, thin white outline) from **March–April 1918**.
- Fabric: varnished plywood (Albatros), streaky green factory dope, or **printed lozenge
  camouflage** (Fokker D.VII), overlaid with vivid personal/unit colours — the "Flying
  Circus" aesthetic.

Marking details live in [markings & liveries](../art-direction/markings-liveries.md).

# Faction feel

Distinctive, colourful, and varied: the twitchy [Dr.I](../aircraft/fokker-dr1.md)
turn-fighter, the forgiving [D.VII](../aircraft/fokker-d7.md) all-rounder, and the
elegant hard-hitting [Albatros](../aircraft/albatros-d3.md). Opposes the
[Allied faction](allied.md).
