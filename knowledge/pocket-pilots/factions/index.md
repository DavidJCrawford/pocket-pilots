# Factions

Pocket Pilots is a two-sided conflict. The player picks any one of the six aircraft;
their side is determined by that aircraft, and they fight the opposing side.

* [Allied (Entente)](allied.md) — Sopwith Camel, SPAD S.XIII, Nieuport 17.
* [Central Powers](central-powers.md) — Fokker Dr.I, Fokker D.VII, Albatros D.III.

**Core rule:** Allied fighters fight Central Powers fighters and vice versa. Enemy AI
is always drawn from the opposing faction's roster — see
[AI opponents](../flight-model/ai-opponents.md).
