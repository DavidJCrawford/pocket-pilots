# Aircraft

The six playable WW1 fighters. Each concept carries real-world specs, the handling
**reputation** that drives flight-model tuning, **silhouette cues** for the low-poly
cartoon model, and **livery** notes for markings.

## Allied (Entente)

* [Sopwith Camel](sopwith-camel.md) — UK rotary biplane; vicious right-turn torque, twin Vickers, the "hump".
* [SPAD S.XIII](spad-s13.md) — French inline energy-fighter; fast, strong diver, poor at low speed.
* [Nieuport 17](nieuport-17.md) — French sesquiplane; nimble climber with a fragile lower wing.

## Central Powers

* [Fokker Dr.I](fokker-dr1.md) — German triplane; supreme agility and climb, slow and poor diver. The Red Baron.
* [Fokker D.VII](fokker-d7.md) — German inline biplane; forgiving, superb at altitude, thick cantilever wings.
* [Albatros D.III](albatros-d3.md) — German sesquiplane; shark-nosed plywood fuselage, twin guns, fragile lower wing.

## How these ground the game

Numeric specs feed [the arcade flight model](../flight-model/arcade-flight-model.md);
handling reputations become per-plane tuning multipliers there. Silhouette cues feed
[the cel-shading art direction](../art-direction/cel-shading.md). Side membership is
defined in [factions](../factions/index.md).
