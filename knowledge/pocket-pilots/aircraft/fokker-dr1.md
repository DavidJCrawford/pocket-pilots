# Real-world profile

The iconic triplane — three stacked wings, welded steel-tube fuselage, rotary engine.
Only ~320 built, but famous far beyond its numbers as **Manfred von Richthofen's**
mount (the "Red Baron"). [^wiki-dr1]

# Handling & reputation

- Superb **agility and climb** with very light, responsive controls — could out-turn
  and out-climb almost anything at low speed. The Central Powers turn-fighter.
- **Noticeably slow in level flight and a poor diver.**
- Early aircraft suffered top-wing structural failures (moisture/build issues) until
  fixed; cramped cockpit, poor forward/downward visibility.

# Silhouette — low-poly cues

- **Three stacked wings** — the single unmistakable feature — thick, deep-chord, with
  small streamlined interplane struts.
- Short stubby fuselage; horseshoe/flat rotary cowling.
- Small comma-shaped rudder with **no fixed fin**.

# Livery & markings

The most famous scheme is **all-red** (von Richthofen). Many Jasta aircraft wore streaky
olive/green factory dope over turquoise undersides with colourful personal markings.
National insignia: **Iron Cross** early, replaced by the straight-armed **Balkenkreuz**
in March–April 1918. See [markings & liveries](../art-direction/markings-liveries.md);
member of the [Central Powers faction](../factions/central-powers.md). The signature red
livery should be the default/hero skin for this plane.
