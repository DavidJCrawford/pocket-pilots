# Real-world profile

An energy fighter built around a powerful water-cooled Hispano-Suiza 8 V8. Flown by
Guynemer, Fonck, and the American Lafayette / 94th "Hat-in-the-Ring" Escadrille. [^wiki-spad]

# Handling & reputation

- Fast, strong, and a superb diver — pilots could dive away from almost anything and
  it would not shed its wings. Good climb.
- **Relatively unmanoeuvrable and treacherous at low speed**; it stalled sharply and
  needed a power-on ("wheel-it-on") landing.
- Rewarded **slashing hit-and-run attacks from altitude** (proto boom-and-zoom), not
  turning circles. The archetypal Allied energy fighter.

# Game tuning inputs

- **Archetype:** Energy fighter — highest top speed and safest dive; mediocre sustained
  turn; sluggish and unforgiving at low speed.
- Relative dials (0–1): TurnRate **0.5**, TopSpeed **0.95**, Climb **0.75**,
  DiveSafety **0.95**, Durability **0.7**, Firepower **0.7**.
- Model a **low-speed penalty**: reduced control authority / higher stall risk below a
  speed threshold, rewarding the player for keeping energy up.

# Silhouette — low-poly cues

- **Boxy, sturdy, slab-sided fuselage** — the robust, upright look versus the delicate
  Nieuport.
- Flat frontal radiator with a rounded/horseshoe nose opening.
- Equal-span wings with prominent **parallel** interplane struts.

# Livery & markings

Often French five-colour camouflage (browns/greens) over pale undersides; some left in
natural doped linen. French **cocarde** (centre→rim: red–white–blue — reversed from the
British order, i.e. blue on the outer ring). Rudder blue-white-red with **blue at the
trailing edge**. Bright unit emblems (e.g. the stork of the Escadrille des Cigognes).
See [markings & liveries](../art-direction/markings-liveries.md); member of the
[Allied faction](../factions/allied.md).
