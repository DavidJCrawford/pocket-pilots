# Art Direction

The look of Pocket Pilots: **bright, plasticky, cartoony cel-shading** — toy WW1 fighters
in a clean blue sky.

* [Cel-shading recipe](cel-shading.md) — the exact Three.js toon + outline technique.
* [Colour palette](color-palette.md) — the plasticky palette, sky, lighting.
* [Markings & liveries](markings-liveries.md) — roundels, cocardes, crosses, lozenge, hero skins.

Grounded technically in [Three.js rendering](../tech-stack/threejs-rendering.md). Applies
to every [aircraft](../aircraft/index.md) model.
